Digiicampus
How sign-in works
Connect opens the university's own sign-in page in a separate window. You sign in there; PCC never sees your password and keeps only the session the site gives you, which lasts about a week.

Mail

PCC scans your inbox for schedule changes and deadlines and lists them on Today for you to approve.

Data

Everything you enter stays on this computer. A backup is saved automatically once a day.
