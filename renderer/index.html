Mail

PCC reads recent mail from your inbox and sorts it by sender on the Inbox page. Everything stays on this computer.

How to get an app password (Gmail and college Google accounts)
  1. An app password is a separate 16-letter password Google makes for one app. Your normal password will not work here.
  2. Turn on 2-Step Verification for the account first: Google Account → Security. App passwords don't exist without it.
  3. Open myaccount.google.com/apppasswords, name it "PCC" and press Create.
  4. Copy the 16 letters into "App password" above and press Save. PCC checks the sign-in and tells you the result.
If that page says the setting isn't available for your account, the college's administrator has switched app passwords off and PCC can't read that mailbox. Other providers (Outlook, Yahoo, Zoho) have their own app-password pages and IMAP server names.
